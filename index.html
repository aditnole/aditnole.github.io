The journey · 2013 → now

Every move asked a bigger question.

Different industries, one thread: start with the data, find the decision it should change, and measure whether it did.

2013 – 2017

Engineering degree · B.Tech

Where it started: optimisation, algorithms for big data, and my first machine-learning course.

The math clicked. I wanted to use it on real problems, not textbook ones.

2017 – 2020 · IndShine

Product Analyst · drones & geospatial

Why a startup: to learn the whole loop, from raw data to pricing to the customer, faster than anywhere else.

Priced services by user segment, built 3D maps from 2D imagery for government projects, and predicted project timelines from historical geospatial data.

5%revenue from segment pricing
20%better timeline accuracy
2020 – 2021 · Netradyne

Data Scientist · driver safety telematics

Why the switch: from analysing data to building models that run on live data, where a missed signal is a real risk on the road.

Built anomaly detection for alert rates and folded new alerts into the driver safety score.

50%faster response to anomalies
2021 – 2022 · JP Morgan Chase

Senior Analyst · equity indices

Why finance: to see how models hold up when every number is money, and to work on NLP for markets.

Built a theme-based index (AI, renewables) using sentiment analysis to classify stocks, plus a volatility-based index for client needs.

$100kclient book value added
2022 – now · Grab

Data Scientist · content moderation & safety

Why Grab: consumer scale across Southeast Asia, and problems where data directly protects people.

Own analytics for content moderation and safety and lead a team of three: risk signals, a BERT profanity model, user segmentation and CLV, and a lot of experimentation.

2×incident detection
10%fewer text incidents, Thailand
2pplower churn
2023 – now · on the side

Founder · SnapMyDesign → CVSwitch → VettaCV

Tailored resumes · matched jobs · real interviewers

Why build: after years of shaping other people's products with data, I wanted to own one end to end, from the model to the checkout page.

Started with an illustration generator, then a resume tailor that grew into VettaCV: jobs matched to your resume, AI interview practice, and real interviewers who can refer you.

400+users this year
+134%active users, 90 days
