01 — Work

What I spend my time on.

01

ML systems

Taking models from a notebook to something that runs every day: the data, the features, serving it, and keeping an eye on it once it's live.

At Grab, fraud models I worked on brought the incident rate down 30 points.

02

AI agents

Building agents with LangGraph and Claude, plus the less glamorous part: measuring when they get things wrong.

A support agent I worked on at Atlassian now handles 50% more questions on its own.

03

Consulting on the fuzzy stuff

Sitting with a team that has a vague question and turning it into something we can measure and act on.

That kind of work fed into safety changes that cut incidents in Thailand by 13%.

04

Experiments & causal inference

A/B and switchback tests, power analysis, causal methods. Mostly in service of one question: did it actually work?

One of these confirmed a 25-point jump in harmful-content detection.

05

Automation

Replacing fragile scripts and manual steps with pipelines that look after themselves.

We moved ~40 legacy SQL jobs to dbt and finished three months ahead of plan.

06

Tools

scikit-learn, PyTorch, LangGraph, Claude, PySpark, dbt, Power BI, Grafana, and a few more.

04 — Side projects

Things I build on the side.

VettaCV · side project

A small app that helps people land their next job.

It's a one-person project these days, so I end up touching everything from the LLM pipeline to the payment page.

  • Rewrites your resume for a specific job, without making anything up
  • Finds openings that fit and helps with the application questions
  • Lets you practise interviews, with AI or with people who've done the job

About 400 people used it this year, from seven countries, and that number has been climbing.
