05 — Side projects

Things I build on the side.

VettaCV · side project

A small app that helps people land their next job.

It's a one-person project these days, so I end up touching everything from the LLM pipeline to the payment page.

  • Rewrites your resume for a specific job, without making anything up
  • Finds openings that fit and helps with the application questions
  • Lets you practise interviews, with AI or with people who've done the job

About 400 people used it this year, from seven countries, and that number has been climbing.
